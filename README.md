# Awesome-Manufacturing-Quality-Management-System

## Top Quality Management System (Manufacturing) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Document Control, CAPA, Change Control, Training, Audits, Nonconformance & ISO/FDA Compliance*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Quality Management Systems (QMS)** in manufacturing. These systems help organizations manage controlled documents, CAPA, deviations, change control, training records, audits, supplier quality, and compliance with standards such as ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and FDA QMSR.



**Examples** include MasterControl, ETQ Reliance, QT9 QMS, Qualio, Arena QMS, Greenlight Guru, Intelex, Sparta TrackWise, Veeva QualityOne, and Qualtrax (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fully featured, validated QMS platforms for regulated manufacturing (especially medical devices and pharma) are predominantly commercial. Practical open options include **OpenQMS-style document/CAPA frameworks**, quality modules inside **Odoo** and **ERPNext**, and lighter document-control or CAPA tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about regulatory gaps.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[MasterControl](https://www.mastercontrol.com/)**  

  Enterprise quality and manufacturing execution platform widely used in life sciences and regulated industries for document control, CAPA, training, and compliance.



- **[ETQ Reliance](https://www.etq.com/)**  

  Flexible QMS platform covering document control, CAPA, change management, audits, and risk across manufacturing and other regulated sectors.



- **[QT9 QMS](https://qt9software.com/)**  

  Cloud quality management system offering document control, CAPA, training, audits, and related modules for manufacturers.



- **[Qualio](https://www.qualio.com/)**  

  Modern eQMS designed for life-science and medtech companies, focusing on document control, training, CAPA, and design controls.



- **[Arena QMS (PTC)](https://www.ptc.com/)**  

  Quality management capabilities integrated with product lifecycle and PLM processes for discrete manufacturers.



- **[Greenlight Guru](https://www.greenlight.guru/)**  

  Medtech-specific eQMS built around design controls, risk management, and alignment with ISO 13485 / FDA QMSR.



- **[Intelex](https://www.intelex.com/)**  

  EHS and quality management platform used by manufacturers for audits, CAPA, document control, and compliance workflows.



- **[Sparta TrackWise (Honeywell)](https://www.honeywell.com/)**  

  Established enterprise QMS used in highly regulated industries for deviation, CAPA, change control, and complaint management.



- **[Veeva QualityOne](https://www.veeva.com/)**  

  Cloud quality management solution within the Veeva Vault ecosystem, popular in life sciences for document and process control.



- **[Qualtrax](https://www.qualtrax.com/)**  

  Compliance and quality management software focused on document control, audits, CAPA, and training for regulated organizations.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Open QMS / GitHub-native QMS frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Emerging open-source approaches that treat QMS processes (document approval, CAPA, change control, training) as version-controlled, reviewable workflows on Git platforms.



- **[Odoo Quality / Manufacturing quality modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source ERP quality management apps covering quality checks, control points, and basic nonconformance workflows integrated with production.



- **[ERPNext Quality Management](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open-source quality features within ERPNext including quality inspections, procedures, and related manufacturing controls.



- **[OpenQMS.net and similar AGPL QMS applications](https://github.com/)**  

  Community and commercial-open QMS applications that provide document control, CAPA, training, and change modules under open licenses.



- **[Document control and wiki-based QMS scaffolds](https://github.com/)**  

  Open templates and tools that implement controlled-document lifecycles using wikis, Git, or simple databases.



- **[CAPA and nonconformance open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open issue-tracking configurations and applications specialized for CAPA, deviations, and corrective actions.



- **[Training record open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open tools for assigning, tracking, and recording employee training related to controlled procedures.



- **[Audit management open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects for planning, executing, and following up on internal and supplier audits.



- **[Risk and FMEA open toolkits](https://github.com/)**  

  Open spreadsheets, Python tools, and templates for risk management and FMEA that can feed into a broader QMS.



- **[ISO / procedure template open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Collections of open procedure and work-instruction templates that organizations adapt for their QMS documentation.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Odoo** or **ERPNext** quality modules when quality processes need tight integration with manufacturing and inventory.

- Exploring **Git-based / Open QMS** approaches for organizations comfortable treating quality records as code and pull requests.

- Combining open document repositories with structured CAPA trackers for smaller or less-regulated manufacturers.

- Accepting that validated, audit-ready eQMS platforms with full 21 CFR Part 11 / Annex 11 support, electronic signatures, and industry-specific best practices still require commercial systems (MasterControl, Greenlight Guru, Qualio, ETQ, Veeva, TrackWise, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency of procedures, lower cost for non-device manufacturers, and data ownership.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage controlled documents and CAPA in an open or hybrid system → link quality events to production data in Odoo/ERPNext → maintain training records → prepare audit packages from version-controlled evidence. Suitable for ISO 9001-focused manufacturers and teams with strong process discipline. Most medical-device, pharma, and highly regulated manufacturers continue to rely on commercial eQMS platforms for compliance and audit readiness.
